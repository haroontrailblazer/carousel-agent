# Cover Style - First Page of Every Carousel

The selected design's cover layout controls its font, colors, sizes, alignment,
logo, handle and element positions. This application serves any creator or
brand. Never impose a particular website palette, account or favicon.

## Format and source media

- 1080 x 1350 px, portrait 4:5.
- Use a sourced 4-15 second video under the runtime duration contract. A sourced
  still with a restrained push-in is the fallback. Cover media is never AI-generated.
- Prefer the announcement, product, event, paper figure or trusted report's
  actual subject. Keep one recognizable focal point when cropping full-bleed.

## Composition

1. Fill the cover edge to edge with the sourced image or trimmed clip.
2. Apply the mandatory black lower shadow: fade from 48% to fully black at
   66% of the canvas, with solid black below. This matches the design editor.
3. Render the plan's hook_title and its verbatim hook_highlight using the
   cover's configured font, title size, position, alignment and exact colors.
4. Add the selected logo and exact handle only when enabled for the cover.
   Contain the logo in its saved box. Left-align and vertically center the
   handle in its own box. Do not replace saved boxes with fixed rail positions.

Keep these layers simple: media, black shadow, title, logo and handle. Do not
add extra grids, arrows, badges, secondary identities or a slide number. The
cover and CTA are unnumbered; body numbering starts at 01.

## Title rules

- Prefer five to seven words, maximum nine, with a clear consequence or idea.
- The highlight must be a verbatim substring of the title.
- No emoji, hashtags or em dashes. Keep the title readable at feed size.
- Do not alter the approved title unless the current review requests it.

## Identity and visual treatment

Use the design's uploaded logo and handle, with the run's account as fallback.
Hidden marks and unbranded runs are valid. Reference media and historical
feedback never supply a substitute identity. Do not tint factual source media
with an unrelated palette or add floating logos, glows or fake evidence.

The cover is composed in code. It does not depend on an external overlay file
or an old brand's reference assets.
