# Studio product illustrations

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. These are photorealistic 3D-style image assets, not interactive 3D models. Original transparent PNGs are preserved in this directory; the interface uses responsive WebP exports in `../../public/illustrations/`.

- `carousel-sculpture.png`: layered carousel cards, used in the creation hero, sign-in, task headings, chat review card, and visual trace agents.
- `research-lens.png`: magnifying glass and paper cards, used for the newsroom, chat activity, and research/planning trace agents.
- `design-stylus.png`: material swatches and stylus, used for settings, the design library, the review caption inspector, and writing/CTA trace agents.

All three have 160px and 320px exports; the hero also has a 640px export. All seven exports total 134,330 bytes (131 KiB); the largest is 55,956 bytes. Original PNGs are outside the public directory and are not shipped in the production bundle.

Illustrations are decorative with adjacent text labels. Supporting images are lazy loaded, dimensions are reserved, and reduced-motion preferences disable entrance animations and hover transitions. Existing trace controls remain keyboard operable. The creation page does not include an agent introduction section.

## Generation prompts

### Carousel sculpture

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: transparent 3D hero cutout for Carousel Factory, an AI carousel creation studio. Create a beautifully art-directed, photorealistic 3D product sculpture of three floating thick portrait carousel cards fanned in a tight compact arrangement. Front card warm porcelain with a large inset burnt-orange abstract arch and two short embossed line shapes; middle card translucent smoked glass with orange edge; back card brushed champagne aluminium. A small polished orange sphere and a thin chrome orbital ring balance the composition. Physically believable thick bevels, fine paper grain, soft studio reflections, ambient occlusion, subtle soft contact shadow. Premium industrial product photography, not cartoon, not toy, not flat icon. Three-quarter view, front card angled slightly left, all objects entirely visible and fill 85% of the frame. Square canvas. Genuine transparent alpha background, no floor, no opaque backdrop, no checkerboard baked into the image. Must work on both warm ivory and pitch-black UI. No text, letters, numbers, branding or watermarks. Restrained palette: burnt orange #C74726, apricot #F79270, ivory #F6F4F0, brushed silver and graphite.

### Research lens

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: transparent 3D research-agent illustration for the Carousel Factory UI. A premium photorealistic small desktop object: a substantial magnifying glass with brushed aluminium rim, optically realistic clear lens and short matte burnt-orange handle, resting diagonally across two small warm-white paper research cards with a few embossed graphite horizontal lines (not readable text). One tiny orange sphere beside it. Physically realistic materials, bevels, refraction and soft contact shadow, warm softbox lighting, three-quarter isometric viewpoint. Object isolated with genuine transparent alpha background, no opaque background, no checkerboard, no floor. Compact square composition filled 85% with the subject; entire object visible. Elegant industrial design, no cartoon faces, no robots, no text, no branding. Palette ivory #F6F4F0, orange #C74726 and #F79270, brushed silver, graphite. Designed to read clearly at 80 pixels on ivory and pitch-black UI.

### Design stylus

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: transparent 3D design-agent illustration for the Carousel Factory UI. A premium photorealistic small desktop sculpture of an orange lacquer and brushed-aluminium precision stylus laying diagonally over two fanned thick material swatch cards, one warm porcelain, one frosted apricot glass, with a small polished graphite sphere beside them. Precision bevels, convincing thickness, restrained surface texture, metal reflections, softly refracted glass edges and soft contact shadow. Three-quarter product photography view, compact square composition, entire object visible filling 85% of the frame. Genuine transparent alpha background; no floor, opaque background or baked checkerboard. No text, letters, logos, watermark or cartoon styling. Palette #C74726 burnt orange, #F79270 apricot, #F6F4F0 ivory, graphite and brushed aluminium. Must remain readable as an 80px UI illustration on both black and ivory.
